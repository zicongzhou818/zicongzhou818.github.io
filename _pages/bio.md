My name is Zicong Zhou, originally from Nanning, China. I came to the US in 2010 for college. I felt that majoring only in Business didn't fit my curiosity about Mother Nature, so I started a dual degree in Mathematics. Fortunately, ever since I started my journey in math, it has become my main passion. After earning my B.A. in Business Administration and B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Maine at Presque Isle in 2012, I started the graduate study. I completed my M.S. and Ph.D. in Mathematics in 2015 and 2019, respectively, from the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA). After finishing my postdoc at the Institute of Natural Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2022, I briefly worked as an (traditional and deep-learning) algorithm developer for Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scanners at Shanghai Siemens Medical Equipment Ltd. Soon, I realized that the corporate ladder was not my calling at this stage of my career. So, I moved back to the DFW area, Texas, and have worked as a research assistant in the Department of Pharmacology and Neuroscience at the University of North Texas Health Science Center (UNTHSC) since 2023. In early 2026, a call of parenting duty has kept me home. Currently, I am working my way back to the math department somewhere so that I can focus more on the mathematical theories of the computational tools that I have been developing for Neuroscience research.
